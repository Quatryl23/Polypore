**Polypore** gives you the ability to put any blocks and items you want on Shelf Mushrooms.
You can deposit one item/block on a small one and two on a large one. In short: Shelf Mushrooms are now proper shelves!

![\---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/3f1ca11c05ba5ce5b0c1dc8582f18c6b061ad9b0.png)

![bookshelf and copper block on a shelf mushroom](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/cfb5794012fa906177245efba51f4c767a9ea5ea.png) 
![potted crimson fungus on a shelf mushroom](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/4bcccb4687eb460c51db4d1d4e4044333835b4b0.png)
![lantern and soul lantern on a shelf mushroom](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a6a7441624e19428255020f0a39123f93024fe50.png)
![diamond sword on a shelf mushroom](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/e13138e967d52b1cdb6c6d1e575d32ec20893707.png)  

Shelf Mushrooms also support some features of blocks put onto them: Light sources emit light (not as much as normal, though) and
flower pots can be filled as if they were placed normally. The hitboxes of most blocks are also adapted by the Shelf Mushroom when placed on them.
